## First Check-In Questions (90 seconds)

*Keep it to 5 questions max. Suggested flow:*

1. **How are you feeling physically today?**
   😩 Rough &nbsp; 😐 Okay &nbsp; 😊 Good &nbsp; 💪 Great

2. **How are you feeling mentally / emotionally?**
   😩 Rough &nbsp; 😐 Okay &nbsp; 😊 Good &nbsp; 💪 Great

3. **How did you sleep last night?**
   😩 Rough &nbsp; 😐 Okay &nbsp; 😊 Good &nbsp; 💪 Great

4. **Is there anything you want your support staff to know?** *(optional)*
   [ text field — optional, goes directly to sports medicine/counseling staff, not coaches ]

5. **Are you doing okay overall?**
   Yes, I'm good &nbsp; / &nbsp; I could use some support

## Notes for Customization

- **Tone:** Peer-level, not clinical. Never say "mental health assessment."
- **Privacy message:** Always lead with what coaches CANNOT see. Trust is built upfront.
- **Frequency:** Daily check-ins are ideal; 3x/week is the minimum for meaningful trend data.
- **Crisis path:** Question 5 triggers a staff alert, not a coach alert. Make sure this is configured correctly before launch.
