# Objection FAQ — Check-In by Athlete Anchor

*For internal use by your champion. Plain answers to the questions you'll get.*

---

**1. "What if athletes don't trust it?"**

> Athletes are told upfront — before submitting anything — exactly what coaches can and cannot see. Coaches only see anonymized team averages, never individual responses. That transparency is the whole design.

---

**2. "What can coaches actually see?"**

> Coaches see a weekly team wellness trend — essentially an average. They cannot see which athlete said what. Individual data is only surfaced if the athlete themselves flags a wellness concern, which they do voluntarily.

---

**3. "Who owns the data?"**

> Your institution owns all data. Athlete Anchor is a processor, not an owner. You can request full deletion at any time and receive written confirmation within 24 hours.

---

**4. "What happens if there's a breach?"**

> We notify your institution within 72 hours of discovery, provide a written incident report within 5 business days, and cooperate fully with your IT and legal teams. Our breach notification policy and email template are included in the pilot package.

---

**5. "How does this comply with FERPA?"**

> Athlete Anchor operates as a "school official" data processor under FERPA. Student wellness data is treated as protected education records. A full FERPA Data Processing Agreement is included in the pilot package and ready to sign.

---

**6. "What if an athlete flags a crisis?"**

> If an athlete submits a response that indicates immediate risk, the platform sends an alert to designated staff (sports medicine or counseling staff — your choice). The athlete is also shown crisis resources immediately in the app. The program defines who receives alerts.

---

**7. "How much time does this take our staff?"**

> Setup is approximately 30 minutes (we do the technical work — you provide a roster CSV). Ongoing time per week is about 5 minutes to review the coach dashboard. There's no new meeting, no new report, no new workflow.

---

**8. "What happens after the pilot ends?"**

> Two options: continue with a paid subscription (pricing TBD based on roster size) or end the pilot. If you end it, we delete all data within 24 hours and send written confirmation. No automatic charges, no lock-in.

---

**9. "How is this different from what we already do?"**

> Most programs rely on coaches noticing something is wrong, or athletes proactively coming forward — both of which consistently miss athletes who are struggling in silence. Check-In gives athletes a private, low-friction channel on their own terms, and gives staff a systematic view instead of relying on observation.

---

**10. "What if we want to cancel?"**

> 24 hours written notice, email is fine. No penalty, no fee, no obligation. We delete everything and send confirmation. That's the whole cancellation process.
