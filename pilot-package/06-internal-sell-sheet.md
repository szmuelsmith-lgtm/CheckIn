# Check-In by Athlete Anchor
## For Your Athletic Director — Internal One-Pager

---

### What It Is

Check-In is a 90-second daily wellness check-in platform for student athletes. Athletes answer a short pulse survey on their phone. Coaches see anonymized team-wide trends. Staff gets alerted only when an athlete flags a concern.

No app download. No new login system. Works on any phone browser.

---

### Why Now

The NCAA's mental health best practices framework places direct responsibility on athletic departments to proactively monitor student athlete wellbeing — not just respond after a crisis. Most programs are still relying on coaches to notice something is wrong.

Check-In gives your staff a systematic, private channel that athletes can actually use.
