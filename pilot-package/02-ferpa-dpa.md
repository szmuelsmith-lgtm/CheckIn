# FERPA Data Processing Agreement — Check-In by Athlete Anchor

**Between:** _________________________ ("Institution") and Athlete Anchor ("Vendor")
**Effective Date:** _______________

---

## 1. Who Owns the Data

The Institution is the data controller. Athlete Anchor is the data processor. All student athlete data is the property of the Institution. Athlete Anchor has no ownership claim over any data collected through the platform.

---

## 2. What We Are Allowed to Do With Your Data

We may use Institution data only to:
- Operate and deliver the Check-In platform
- Send wellness alerts to authorized staff
- Generate anonymized aggregate reports for authorized coaches and administrators
- Troubleshoot technical issues at your request

We may NOT use Institution data to:
- Train machine learning models
- Build profiles for advertising or any commercial purpose
- Share with any third party except the subprocessors listed below
- Retain data after the agreement ends

---

## 3. FERPA Acknowledgment

Athlete Anchor acknowledges that student wellness data collected through Check-In constitutes education records under FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g). Athlete Anchor agrees to:
- Access student records only as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest as defined by FERPA
- Not re-disclose student records without written consent from the Institution
- Return or destroy student records upon termination of this agreement
- Comply with all applicable FERPA requirements as directed by the Institution

---

## 4. Subprocessors

We use two subprocessors. No student data leaves US infrastructure.

| Vendor | Purpose | Region | Security Page |
|--------|---------|--------|--------------|
| Supabase (AWS us-east-1) | Database and authentication | United States | supabase.com/security |
| Vercel | Application hosting | United States | vercel.com/security |

We will notify the Institution at least 14 days before adding any new subprocessor.

---

## 5. Breach Notification

If we discover or reasonably suspect a data breach affecting Institution data, we will:
- Notify the Institution within **72 hours** of discovery via email and phone
- Provide a written incident report within 5 business days
- Cooperate fully with any investigation
- Notify affected individuals as directed by the Institution

---

## 6. Data Deletion on Termination

Within 24 hours of written request or agreement termination, we will permanently delete:
- All check-in records
- All journal entries
- All athlete and staff profiles
- All alerts and follow-up records
- All coach notes

We will send written confirmation when deletion is complete.

---

## 7. Security Standards

Athlete Anchor maintains: encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+), row-level data isolation between institutions, and multi-factor authentication for all administrative access.
