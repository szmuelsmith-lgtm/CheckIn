# Breach Notification Policy — Check-In by Athlete Anchor

---

## Our Commitment

If we discover or reasonably suspect that student athlete data has been accessed, disclosed, or compromised without authorization, we notify affected institutions within **72 hours** of discovery — no exceptions.

---

## What We Do Immediately Upon Discovery

**Within the first hour:**
- Isolate the affected system or account
- Revoke any potentially compromised credentials
- Preserve logs for forensic review
- Document what we know and what we don't

**Within 24 hours:**
- Notify all affected institution contacts by email and phone
- Provide an initial incident summary (what happened, what data, what we've done)
- Establish a dedicated communication channel for updates

**Within 72 hours:**
- Deliver a written incident report to all affected institutions
- Include: scope of breach, data categories affected, number of records, root cause (if known), remediation steps taken

**Within 5 business days:**
- Provide final incident report with full timeline
- Recommend any follow-up actions for the institution
- Notify affected individuals if directed by the institution

---

## What We Tell You

Every breach notification includes:
- Date and time of discovery
- Nature of the incident (unauthorized access, data exposure, etc.)
- Categories of data affected (check-ins, profiles, journal entries, etc.)
- Approximate number of records
- What we did to contain it
- What we're doing to prevent recurrence
- Contact name and direct phone number for questions

---

## FERPA Alignment

FERPA does not specify a breach notification timeline, but directs institutions to handle breaches according to their own policies. We notify within 72 hours so your institution has maximum time to meet any state breach notification laws (which typically require 30–72 hour notice to affected individuals).

We defer to the institution on whether and how to notify individual student athletes.

---

## Ongoing Updates

After initial notification, we send status updates every 24 hours until the incident is fully resolved. You will always know where things stand.

---

## Remediation

After any breach:
- We conduct a full security review
- We share findings with affected institutions
- We implement fixes before restoring affected services
- We provide a written post-mortem within 10 business days
