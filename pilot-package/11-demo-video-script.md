# Demo Video Script — Check-In by Athlete Anchor
## 3-Minute Screen Recording

*Tone: calm, peer-level, not a pitch. You're showing something that already works.*
*Audience: Athletic Director and sports medicine staff.*
*Goal: They agree to a pilot before you're even in the room.*

---

## SECTION 1 — The Problem (0:00–0:30)

**[Screen: blank or simple slide — just your face / voiceover over a blank screen]**

**Say:**
> "Most athletic programs find out an athlete is struggling in one of two ways — a coach notices something, or the athlete reaches out themselves. Both of those miss the athletes who are quietly falling behind.
>
> Check-In gives athletes a private, 90-second channel to tell you how they're actually doing — and gives your staff a system to catch it early, before it becomes a crisis."

---

## SECTION 2 — Athlete Check-In (0:30–1:30)

**[Screen: open a mobile browser window. Navigate to the athlete login page.]**

**Say:**
> "This is what an athlete sees. No app download, no account creation — they get one email with a link, they tap it, they're in."

**[Click the magic link → lands on the check-in screen]**

**Say:**
> "The first thing they see is exactly what their coaches can and cannot see. We put that upfront on purpose — trust is built before they submit anything."

**[Scroll through the privacy notice — pause 2 seconds]**

**Say:**
> "Then five questions. Physical. Mental. Sleep. Anything you want staff to know — that one's optional. And a simple flag if they need support."

**[Click through the 5 questions, selecting mid-range answers]**

**Say:**
> "The whole thing takes about 90 seconds. Works on any phone browser."

**[Click submit → confirmation screen appears]**

**Say:**
> "Done. They see a confirmation. If they flagged that they need support, the right staff member gets an alert — not the coach."

---

## SECTION 3 — Coach Dashboard (1:30–2:15)

**[Screen: navigate to the coach dashboard view]**

**Say:**
> "This is what a coach sees. Not individual answers — team trends."

**[Show the weekly trend chart — highlight the anonymized averages]**

**Say:**
> "They can see that the team's average sleep score dropped this week, or that mental wellness is down heading into a travel week. They cannot see which athlete said what."

**[Hover over an athlete card — show that individual names show no individual data]**

**Say:**
> "Individual athletes only appear if they've flagged a concern. The coach sees that someone on the roster requested support — they don't see the specific response, and they're directed to loop in sports medicine."

**[Emphasize — pause]**

**Say:**
> "This is the privacy design. Athletes open up when they know coaches don't have access to their personal responses. That's what makes the data real."

---

## SECTION 4 — Admin Alerts View (2:15–3:00)

**[Screen: navigate to the admin / sports medicine alerts view]**

**Say:**
> "Your sports medicine and counseling staff see the full picture — including flagged responses."

**[Show an alert card — name visible, response visible, follow-up status]**

**Say:**
> "When an athlete flags that they could use support, it shows up here. Staff can mark it as reviewed, log a follow-up, and track whether it was resolved."

**[Show the follow-up log]**

**Say:**
> "Nothing falls through the cracks. If someone flags a concern and no one follows up in 24 hours, it stays marked as open."

**[Navigate to the admin overview — total athletes, response rate, open alerts]**

**Say:**
> "And at the program level, you can see response rate, team wellness over time, and any open items — everything you'd want for your NCAA documentation."

---

## CLOSING (at 3:00 — no on-screen content needed)

**Say:**
> "The pilot is 60 days, completely free, and you can cancel with 24 hours notice. I handle all the setup — you give me a roster and I'll have everyone ready to check in before your first practice.
>
> If you're open to trying it, I'll send over the one-page pilot agreement and we can move in a week."

---

## Production Notes

- **Record on mobile simulator** or a real phone screen-mirrored — the mobile-first experience is a selling point
- **Use real-looking test data** — not "Test Athlete 1." Give athletes real-sounding names in your demo environment
- **Keep the pace slow** — pause after each section. Let them absorb.
- **No background music.** Calm and professional.
- **File name when sharing:** `CheckIn-Demo-[School Name]-[Date].mp4` — personalize it
