## What This Is

Athlete Anchor is giving your program free access to Check-In, an athlete wellness check-in platform, for 60 days. This agreement explains how it works and protects both sides.

---

## The Pilot

- **Length:** 60 days from the date both parties sign
- **Cost:** $0.00. No invoice, no credit card, no obligation to pay anything.
- **Access:** We will create accounts for your staff and athletes. You provide the roster.
- **Support:** Direct access to the founder (Samuel Smith) for the duration of the pilot.

---

## Your Data

- **You own everything.** All athlete data — check-ins, journal entries, wellness scores — belongs to your institution.
- We process your data only to deliver the platform. We do nothing else with it.
- We do not sell, share, license, or analyze your data for any purpose outside of running the service.
- Your data is stored in the United States (Supabase, AWS us-east-1).

---

## Cancellation

Either party can cancel at any time with 24 hours written notice (email is fine). No penalty, no fee, no questions.

---

## Data Deletion

When the pilot ends or if you cancel:
- We will delete all athlete data, coach data, check-ins, journal entries, and any associated records within **24 hours** of your written request.
- We will send you a written confirmation once deletion is complete.

---

## Privacy

- We are FERPA-compliant. We treat all student athlete data as protected education records.
- Athletes interact with the platform voluntarily.
- Coaches see only aggregated, anonymized wellness trends — never individual responses unless an athlete flags a crisis.

---

## Liability

This is a free pilot. Athlete Anchor is not liable for any indirect or consequential damages. Our total liability is capped at $0 (the amount you paid us).

---

## That's It

If something comes up that isn't covered here, we'll work it out directly. The goal is a good pilot, not a complicated contract.

> **⚠ LAWYER REVIEW FLAGS**
> Before using in an actual pilot, have a real attorney review:
> 1. **Liability cap at $0** — courts in some states won't enforce this. Consider a nominal cap (e.g., $100) instead.
> 2. **FERPA compliance acknowledgment** — your DPA should be a separate addendum countersigned by a school official with authority to sign FERPA agreements (not just any administrator).
> 3. **Jurisdiction / governing law clause** — this agreement doesn't specify which state's law governs a dispute. Add a clause (suggest: Florida, where you're incorporated).
